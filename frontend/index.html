Clinical Screening Selection Philosophy

In healthcare screening, failing to flag a patient with a subclinical disease (False Negative) is significantly more detrimental than calling in a healthy person for confirmation (False Positive). Therefore, model selection prioritizes Recall (Sensitivity) and ROC-AUC over raw accuracy using a composite weighting: Composite Score = 0.40 × Recall + 0.30 × ROC-AUC + 0.15 × F1 + 0.15 × Accuracy

Crucial Medical Scope & Educational Disclaimer

HealthPredict is strictly an educational and screening tool designed for subclinical risk estimation and lifestyle analytics. It does NOT provide medical diagnoses, prescribe treatment, or substitute for comprehensive clinical evaluation by a licensed physician.

Technical Architecture & Defense FAQ

Q: Why are separate ML pipelines used instead of a single multi-label model?

Each disease has distinct physiological biomarkers and etiology. Merging disparate biomedical datasets into one multi-target model causes severe feature sparsity and imputation artifacts. Separate specialized pipelines fitted with domain feature engineering guarantee maximum predictive fidelity.

Q: How does SHAP prevent the model from acting as a 'black box'?

SHAP computes additive Shapley values based on cooperative game theory, proving mathematically how each biomarker (e.g. glucose, MAP, ST depression) influenced the model's log-odds output relative to baseline cohort distributions.

Q: How is data leakage prevented during preprocessing?

All data transformations (median imputation, standard scaling) are encapsulated strictly within Scikit-Learn Pipeline objects fitted exclusively on the training folds, avoiding any information leakage to validation/test folds.
